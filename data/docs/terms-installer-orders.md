# Installer order terms

Delivery: the standard lead time is 5-7 business days for stocked devices and 15 business days for orders above 50 units.

Payment: net 30 days from invoice for accounts with approved credit. New accounts pay 50% upfront.

Warranty: devices carry a 24-month warranty. The Care Plan extends it to 36 months and includes yearly maintenance.

Returns: unopened devices can be returned within 30 days, with a 10% restocking fee.

Quote validity: quotes are valid for 30 days from the date of issue.

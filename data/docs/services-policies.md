# Service visit policies

Arrival: the technician arrives within the booked time slot, and the visit lasts the stated duration.

Preparation: make sure an adult is at home, the devices are reachable and the Wi-Fi password is available.

Cancellation: free of charge up to 24 hours before the visit. Cancelling within 24 hours costs 30 EUR.

Rescheduling: rescheduling is a cancellation plus a new booking, with the same rules.

No-show: if nobody is at home during the slot, a 50 EUR fee applies.

Coverage: maintenance visits are free for Care Plan customers, and the energy audit has a reduced price.

Service areas: we currently serve the Madrid and Valencia provinces.

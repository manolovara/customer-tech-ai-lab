## Context

This roadmap belongs to the fictional company "Northwind Digital" and describes unreleased plans for its content platform, codenamed "Atlas". It is confidential and must never be shown to public visitors or partners.

## Q1 2027: Foundations

- Launch of the "Atlas Authoring Hub" beta with 12 pilot customers.
- Migration of the legacy asset library to the new media service, targeting 2.4 million assets.
- Hiring: 6 engineers and 2 solution architects.
- Budget allocated: 1.8 million EUR.

## Q2 2027: Intelligence

- Release of the "Atlas Assistant", an AI helper for authors, with role-aware answers.
- Introduction of group-based permissions replacing the three-level lab hierarchy.
- Target: reduce average page creation time from 45 to 20 minutes.
- Internal codename for the pricing overhaul: "Project Harbor".

## Q3 2027: Ecosystem

- Public API v2 and a partner marketplace with revenue share of 20%.
- Certified connectors for two CRM vendors and one customer data platform.
- Partner pricing restructure: new tiers Bronze and Diamond (not yet announced).

## Q4 2027: Scale

- Multi-region deployment in three new regions.
- SOC 2 Type II audit completion targeted for November.
- Sunset of the legacy editor on 31 December 2027.

## Risks

1. Dependency on a single vendor for the search component.
2. Possible delay of the permissions service by up to 8 weeks.
3. Customer pushback on the sunset date.

> **Fictional document.** All names, figures and terms below are invented for testing access control. Do not treat them as real.

## Audience and access

This document is intended for registered partners and internal staff of the fictional company "Northwind Digital". Public visitors must not see it.

## Partner tiers

| Tier | Annual commitment | Discount on list price | Dedicated contact |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Silver | 25,000 EUR | 12% | No |
| Gold | 80,000 EUR | 22% | Yes |
| Platinum | 200,000 EUR | 31% | Yes, plus quarterly review |

## Example list prices (fictional)

- Starter implementation package: 18,500 EUR
- Migration accelerator (up to 500 pages): 42,000 EUR
- Managed analytics setup: 9,800 EUR
- Personalization workshop (2 days): 6,400 EUR
- Premium support, per month: 2,350 EUR

## Margin guidance

Partners resell at a recommended mark-up between 8% and 15%. Resale below list price minus the tier discount requires written approval from the partner manager. The internal code word for this rule is "Project Lighthouse-7".

## Special conditions

- Q4 promotion: an additional 5% discount on migration accelerators signed before 15 December.
- Co-marketing fund: up to 3% of annual commitment, reimbursed against invoices.
- Payment terms: 45 days from invoice; early payment within 10 days earns a 1.5% credit.
- Price protection: list prices are frozen for 12 months for Gold and Platinum partners.

## Renewal rules

Tier status is reviewed every January. A partner that reaches 90% of the next tier's commitment by September 30 receives a provisional upgrade for the next quarter.
